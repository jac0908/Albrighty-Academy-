About Albrighty Academy

Located in the heart of Shegar City, Guje Gafarsa, Kella, Albrighty Academy has been a beacon of educational excellence since 2009, serving students from Grade 1 through Grade 8.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Grades 1-8

What are the admission requirements for Grade 1?
For Grade 1 admission, child must be 6 years old by September 1. Required documents: birth certificate, previous school records (if any), passport photos, and completed application form.
What is the maximum class size?
We maintain small class sizes with a maximum of 25 students per class to ensure individual attention for every student in Grades 1-8.
Do you provide transportation?
Yes, we provide safe and reliable school bus transportation for students. Routes cover Shegar City and surrounding areas.
What is the late registration penalty?
After April 15, 2024, a late fee of 500 ETB will be added to the regular registration fee of 5,000 ETB, making the total 5,500 ETB.
